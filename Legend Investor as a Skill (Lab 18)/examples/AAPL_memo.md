I like businesses that turn reported profit into cash their owners can use. Apple passes the specified return-on-equity, positive owner-earnings, and beginning-to-end growth checks. The owner earnings proxy rose from USD 111,443,000,000 at fiscal year end 2022-09-24 to USD 136,056,000,000 at fiscal year end 2026-09-26. The path was uneven: the proxy was USD 98,767,000,000 at fiscal year end 2025-09-27. These are substantial cash results, but they do not promise smooth compounding.

The annual filing describes an integrated hardware, software, and services offering and an ecosystem that supports its competitive position. That suggests a plausible source of customer attachment. The same filing describes aggressive competition and the need for continuing innovation. I would investigate the durability of customer attachment before treating the moat as established. [Annual business and competition discussion](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000026/aapl-20260926.htm)

The quarterly filing describes commercial paper financing used for general corporate purposes, including dividends and repurchases. That deserves scrutiny alongside capital allocation; the limited passages do not establish that management deserves an owner's unconditional trust. [Quarterly financing and repurchase discussion](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019326000020/aapl-20260627.htm)

ROE was 117.92% at fiscal year end 2026-09-26. A small equity denominator can magnify that ratio, so I would not mistake a high ratio for proof of an impregnable franchise. Owner earnings here subtracts total capital spending from operating cash flow; it is a proxy, not a maintenance-investment estimate or Buffett's complete calculation.

My uncomfortable question is whether the ecosystem can keep customers loyal while innovation and competition demand continuing investment. Evidence of durable customer retention and disciplined capital allocation would move me toward owning the business. Erosion in either, or sustained deterioration in cash generation, would move me toward passing. This memo judges the business only and makes no judgment about its share price.
