# COST — Costco business-quality memo

Buffett-inspired AI analysis, not a statement by Warren Buffett or Berkshire Hathaway.

Decision: own the business

I would be comfortable owning this business on the evidence supplied. Costco's simple bargain is attractive: deliver value to members, turn inventory quickly, and use purchasing and distribution efficiency to keep costs low. My inference is that this operating system supports a cost advantage and member trust. The annual filing describes these mechanisms; it does not independently prove that the advantage will endure. [Business model and operating practices](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000093/cost-20260830.htm)

The script's return-on-equity, positive owner-earnings, and beginning-to-end owner-earnings growth checks all pass. At fiscal year end 2022-08-28, the owner earnings proxy was USD 3,501,000,000; at fiscal year end 2026-08-30, it was USD 9,382,000,000. The latest net income was USD 9,226,000,000 and ROE was 25.77%. The intervening owner earnings record was not uniformly rising, so I would not confuse the endpoint improvement with uninterrupted growth.

The quarterly filing's stated willingness to hold down merchandise prices despite cost increases supports the member-value philosophy. I regard that as consistent with protecting the franchise rather than extracting the largest immediate margin. It remains management's description of its conduct, and these excerpts are insufficient for a complete assessment of compensation, incentives, or capital allocation. [Pricing philosophy and competitive conditions](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000051/cost-20260510.htm)

My chief concern is that the model operates on thin merchandise margins: cost pressure or loss of pricing credibility could weaken the economics. I would change my mind if evidence showed that members no longer receive compelling value, inventory efficiency deteriorates, or cash generation persistently weakens. Additional evidence about member retention and management's allocation decisions would strengthen or undermine this judgment.

Owner earnings here is operating cash flow minus total capital spending, a proxy rather than Buffett's complete owner-earnings calculation. High ROE can also reflect a small equity denominator. This is a favorable business-quality verdict only; share-price attractiveness has not been assessed.

Verdict confidence: I provisionally trust the verdict because the financial checks and Costco’s operating model support business quality, though the limited evidence leaves moat durability and management quality uncertain.
