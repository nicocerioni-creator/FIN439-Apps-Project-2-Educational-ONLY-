# COST — Buffett-inspired business-quality memo

Educational AI analysis inspired by Warren Buffett; not an actual statement by Buffett or Berkshire Hathaway.

Costco earns a favorable business-quality judgment because its low-price membership model links purchasing scale, rapid inventory turnover, and distribution efficiency to member value. The annual filing describes this operating system; my inference is that it supports a cost advantage and customer trust. It does not prove that the advantage will endure. [Operating model](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000093/cost-20260830.htm)

The coded ROE, positive owner-earnings, and endpoint owner-earnings growth checks all pass. Owner earnings rose from USD 3,501,000,000 at fiscal year end 2022-08-28 to USD 9,382,000,000 at fiscal year end 2026-08-30. Latest net income was USD 9,226,000,000 and ROE was 25.77%. The endpoint improvement does not mean owner earnings increased in every intervening year.

Management describes holding merchandise prices steady despite cost increases when protecting member value requires it. I infer that this discipline can protect the franchise, although the excerpts cannot establish management quality or the merits of every capital-allocation decision. [Pricing philosophy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/909832/000090983226000051/cost-20260510.htm)

The biggest uncertainty is whether competitive pressure or cost inflation weakens the member-value proposition and cash generation. I would change my judgment if member value deteriorated or the cash record weakened persistently. Owner earnings here subtracts total capital spending from operating cash flow; it is a proxy, not Buffett's complete owner-earnings definition. High ROE can also reflect a small year-end equity denominator.

I provisionally trust this business-quality verdict because the coded evidence and operating model agree, while the limited qualitative evidence leaves moat durability uncertain. No share price or valuation was assessed, so this is not a judgment that COST shares are attractive at any price.
