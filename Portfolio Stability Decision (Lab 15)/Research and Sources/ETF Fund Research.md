## Portfolio interpretation

**More sector funds do not automatically mean more diversification.** VOO and SCHB already own companies across these sectors. Adding sector funds increases the portfolio's weight in selected industries.

- **VOO and SCHB:** retain both on the research list as requested, but recognize that they perform similar core roles and overlap substantially.
- **QQQ:** already provides substantial technology exposure. Adding AIQ or SMH is an intentional increase in technology exposure rather than a broad diversification move.
- **SCHD:** provides a dividend/value emphasis. Dividends do not guarantee higher total returns or protect principal.
- **PAVE, XLE, and XLV:** offer the clearest changes in emphasis alongside the four retained funds, covering infrastructure development, traditional energy, and healthcare.
- **GRID:** adds a more specific electrification angle. Its connection to AI power demand is an investment thesis, not a guarantee of higher returns.
- **AIQ versus SMH:** AIQ covers a broader AI theme, while SMH focuses on semiconductor production and equipment. Neither is a pure investment in AI revenues alone.

## Next research step

Before selecting weights, compare the funds' current holdings and combined sector exposure against the existing portfolio, then consider investment timeline and tolerance for losses. Evaluate return alongside concentration, volatility, and fees rather than selecting solely on recent performance.
